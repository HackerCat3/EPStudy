Canvas Token Setup Guide

Follow these exact steps in Canvas, then paste the token into Canvas Sync. Keep this token private and never share it.

  1. In Canvas, click your profile avatar/initials in the left nav.
  2. Click Settings from the account menu.
  3. Scroll to Approved Integrations and click + New Access Token.
  4. Set Purpose to EPStudy and choose an expiration date/time.
  5. Click Generate Token.
  6. Copy the token immediately from Access Token Details.
  7. In EPStudy, enter domain as your school's URL and paste token into the field.
  8. Keep Use private proxy bypass checked, then click Sync.
