Welcome To EPS Workspace

Here is how to get the most out of your dashboard:

  • Manage Tasks: Add your homework and let the Smart Suggestion pick what you should tackle next.
  • Stay Focused: Use the built-in Pomodoro timer to log focus sessions and build your daily streak.
  • Sync Data: Go to the Settings tab to link your Canvas and TeamSnap accounts.
  • Unlock Skins: Complete tasks and focus sessions to unlock new background themes and features.

How to get your Canvas Token

Follow these steps to securely link your assignments.

  1. Log into Canvas and click your Account (Profile Avatar) in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Settings.
  3. Scroll down to "Approved Integrations" and click the + New Access Token button.
  4. Enter a purpose (e.g., "EPS Workspace") and click Generate Token.
  5. Copy the long token code provided and paste it into the Settings page here.
